What the redesign changes — layout & styling only

1

Advice, not just a list

The Start/Sit column now leads with the verdict — "you're leaving 8.4 points on the bench" and the exact swaps to fix it, with win-odds impact — instead of making you scan every card to find the tension.

2

A FAAB budget you can see

Your remaining FAAB sits in the header as a bar and a number, and each target shows a low·target·stretch bid band. Today the budget is invisible and bids hide behind a toggle.

3

Cleaner waiver rows

Rank, name, and the "why add" signal read first; Value and FAAB sit in aligned metric columns with tabular figures so the whole list scans down cleanly.

4

Recommended starter, highlighted

The player the advisor wants you to start gets a green ring in his position group, tying the swap banner to the actual lineup card. Byes and injuries stay dimmed / flagged.

5

Same components, both themes & sizes

Position pills, badges, matchup and Vegas chips, the compare panel — all your existing pieces, on your real tokens. Toggle light/dark and desktop/mobile above.
