What the redesign changes — layout & styling only

1

Desktop finally uses the width

Today the page is a 720px column centered in a sea of empty desktop space. The redesign splits it into a wide play feed plus a sticky rail (matchups, on-deck, top performers) — nothing new, just placed where a big screen can see it at once.

2

Two-up feed on desktop

The live feed flows into two columns on wide screens, so twice as many scoring plays are visible without scrolling. On mobile it stays a single stacked list.

3

Same components, calmer surface

Your real tokens, Archivo/Inter type, the red identity line, amber-TD / green-gain / red-injury left borders, position-tinted avatars — all kept. Just more consistent spacing and one radius scale.

4

Win-share bar on every matchup

Each matchup card gets the same advantage bar the hero already uses, so lead size reads at a glance across the league board, not just for your game.

5

Both themes, both viewports

Toggle light/dark and desktop/mobile above — the redesign is built on your theme variables, so it tracks the app's existing light and dark palettes exactly.
