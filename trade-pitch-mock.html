3. Tone variations

Three pre-written tones. Kaedon picks one as the default.

A. Casual (default)
Hey! Saw you're 2-5 and thin at WR. Would you do Nico Collins for Zay Flowers? Collins gives you a locked-in WR1 this week and I'm betting on Flowers' upside rest of season. Pretty even on value. Lmk what you think!
B. Straightforward
Interested in Zay Flowers? I'd send Nico Collins. You're 2-5 and need WR help now, Collins starts for you immediately. Values line up about even. Let me know.
C. Detailed
Hey, looking at your roster (2-5, rebuilding, thin at WR) I think there's a deal here: my Nico Collins for your Zay Flowers. Collins is the steadier weekly starter which helps you now, Flowers has the bigger ceiling which fits my window. Trade database has this shape getting accepted 63% of the time and our values are within 5%. Open to a counter if you're close.
Each pitch pulls from the card data: player names, fairness label, accept rate, partner record, partner needs, partner direction. The "why it helps them" line is the key part the site knows and the user usually fumbles.
