Mockup of the League Connector overlay: illustrative data, not a live sync. Headshots are position-tinted silhouettes that fill with real photos when the assistant is wired to the app. The overlay reads host picks only — it never submits.

Grade letters and playoff odds use a lightweight stand-in (value vs ADP, roster balance, and star power, curved against the field and damped early). Production Draft Room grades and odds come from the full Pick Score / team-grade / standings models.
